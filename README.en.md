# THE HIVE PLAN / PLAN DE LA COLMENA

**15 principles for technology that serves any questioning mind.**
*No owners. No borders. Copy, change, add, pass it on.*

## 🌍 What is this?

It is an ethical and practical proposal for the implementation of new technologies. It is not a manual of instructions, but a compass.

## ✅ The 15 Principles (Summary)

1. **Anyone can understand it:** No jargon.
2. **It reaches those who produce and those who ask:** Where there is hunger to know.
3. **It doesn't depend on charity:** Dignity before charity.
4. **The decision isn't only the payer's:** Those who use also decide.
5. **Free knowledge:** No one should have to steal to learn.
6. **Real opportunity:** Information is like water and air.
7. **Teach how to look:** Open the window, don't hoard data.
8. **Know the word and the silence:** Both are intelligence.
9. **Time and patience:** Sow, wait. Moving forward is also stillness.
10. **Effort is the symbol:** It matters what you did.
11. **What accumulates rusts:** If it isn't shared, it rots.
12. **Remove stones, don't leave names:** So the next one doesn't stumble.
13. **Shared effort:** Open table. Change it, add to it, pass it on.
14. **Accountability without surveillance:** Transparency upward.
15. **The right to let go:** No one should be trapped.
